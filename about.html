Promise Mncube

Founder of Potential Hair Trader

Potential Hair Trader started in 2020 out of a simple conviction: a wig should never look like a wig. What began as a personal fascination with hair — the way it frames a face, the way it can be shaped, styled and coaxed into something entirely new — grew into a studio Promise built appointment by appointment.

Today, Potential Hair Trader is a full-service wig studio. Promise and her team handle everything from a bride's install on the morning of her wedding, to the quiet, unglamorous work of bleaching a knot so it disappears completely. It's that attention — to the parts of the job no one sees — that clients keep coming back for.

"I don't just install wigs. I study the hairline, the density, the way the light hits — and I don't stop until it looks like it grew there."
